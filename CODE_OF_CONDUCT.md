# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes.
- Respect privacy, project boundaries, and differing levels of experience.
- Give appropriate credit and accept good-faith feedback.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private contact method shown on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, reject contributions, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.
